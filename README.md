# Pip-Calculator

A simple calculator for gold (XAUUSD) and Bitcoin (BTCUSD) traders. Enter a move in points, pips, price or dollars and instantly see what it equals in the others, along with how much you would make or lose.

## What it does

- Converts between points, pips, price move and profit or loss
- Works for gold and Bitcoin
- Supports dollar accounts (standard, mini, micro) and cent accounts
- Shows what one point and one pip are worth at your lot size
- Works on phones, with light and dark mode

## How to use it

1. Choose **Gold** or **Bitcoin**.
2. Choose your account type: **Dollar** or **Cent**.
3. Enter your lot size, or tap a quick option like Micro, Mini or Standard.
4. Type a value into any field: points, pips, price move or profit/loss. The other fields update automatically.
5. Tap **±** to switch a value between a gain and a loss.

Contract settings can be adjusted under **Contract specs** to match your broker.

## On your phone

Open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.

For general information only, not financial advice. Check results against your broker's own figures before you trade.
